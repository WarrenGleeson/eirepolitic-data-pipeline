## Example treatments

### Broadcasting (Amendment) Bill 2026, 54/2026

Evidence density: high.

Treatment: two slides.

1. Bill summary with WHAT THE BILL DOES, WHAT HAPPENED AT THIRD STAGE, KEY AMENDMENT ACTIVITY, PRACTICAL EFFECT and WHERE IT IS NOW / WHAT HAPPENS NEXT.
2. Dedicated amendment activity slide showing examples of ruled-out, agreed, withdrawn and lost amendment outcomes, including the certified 4 Tá / 5 Níl amendment division. The vote is explicitly framed as an amendment vote, not a vote on the Bill as a whole.

### Social Welfare, Civil Registration and Charities (Amendment) Bill 2026, 84/2026

Evidence density: light to medium.

Official Bill page shows Dáil Third Stage current from 22 September 2026 and links the initiated Bill, Second Stage and referral to select committee. The audit did not locate a published Committee Stage debate/amendment record linked from the Bill page.

Treatment: one compact slide. Explain what the Bill does, the exact Third Stage position, what evidence is currently available, and what should be rechecked before publication. Do not invent amendment activity.

### Strategic Lawsuits against Public Participation Bill 2026, 76/2026

Evidence density: developing.

Official Oireachtas listings show the Bill remains before Dáil Éireann. The audit located its Second Stage/referral context and scheduled committee consideration, but no published Committee Stage amendment/vote record sufficient for an outcome-focused slide.

Treatment: one compact, explicitly provisional slide. Explain the Bill, current procedural position, and that the committee record must be rechecked before final publication.

## Design conclusion

Third Stage should not impose one fixed Bill-slide count. Use one compact slide when the published committee record is thin, and add a second amendment/division slide only when the official record contains enough substantive activity to justify it.
